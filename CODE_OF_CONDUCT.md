# 行为守则 / Code of Conduct

## 中文

本项目采用 [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 的约定，下面是适用本仓库的中文版。

### 我们的承诺

作为成员、贡献者和维护者，我们承诺让参与本项目的体验不带骚扰，无论年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济状况、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性身份与性取向。

我们承诺以有助于营造开放、友好、多元、包容和健康社区的方式行事和互动。

### 我们的标准

有助于营造积极环境的行为包括：

- 对他人展现同理心和善意
- 尊重不同的意见、观点和经验
- 给予并优雅地接受建设性反馈
- 承担责任，向受我们错误影响的人道歉，并从中吸取教训
- 关注的不仅是我们个人，还有整个社区的最佳利益

不可接受的行为包括：

- 使用性化的语言或图像，以及任何形式的性关注或挑逗
- 钓鱼、侮辱或贬损性评论，以及人身或政治攻击
- 公开或私下的骚扰
- 未经明确许可发布他人的私人信息，如真实姓名、住址、邮箱或站点账号
- 其他在专业环境中可被合理认为不恰当的行为

### 适用范围

本守则适用于所有项目空间，也适用于个人在公共空间正式代表项目时。

### 执行

辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为，可通过仓库的 Issues 或私下联系维护者举报。所有投诉都会被及时、公正地审查和调查。

维护者有义务尊重举报人的隐私和安全。执行细则参照 Contributor Covenant v2.1「执行指南」，按情节轻重采取更正、警告、临时封禁至永久封禁等措施。

## English

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), reproduced below for this repository.

### Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

### Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, or site account, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

### Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported via the repository's Issues or privately to the maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant v2.1 Enforcement Guidelines, escalating from correction and warning to temporary or permanent ban.
